# Kept — Final Video Dialogue Script (Nidhi & Dad)

### Part 1 • The Problem & Thesis
**NIDHI:**  
"Alexa lives in over 100 million homes. But the second you ask her for help... she acts like a complete stranger."

**DAD:**  
"She answers questions and recommends products, completely blind to our family and our home."

**NIDHI:**  
"Because the AI living on our kitchen counter shouldn’t act like a stranger. Kept connects Alexa to what matters most—our safety, our learning, and our home."

---

### Part 2 • Scenario 1: Food & Allergy Shield
**NIDHI:**  
"Let’s watch how this works in real life.

One day, Dad asked Alexa for dinner for my 5-year-old brother, Leo. Alexa cheerfully recommended walnut pesto. But Leo has a severe nut allergy!

Because Kept has our family facts safely remembered, it stopped it instantly: *'Nut allergy detected.'* In one tap—safe crispy chicken tenders, made for our Ninja Air Fryer."

---

### Part 3 • Scenario 2: Age-Grounded Learning
**NIDHI:**  
"On another day, I asked Alexa *'Why do stars explode?'*, and generic AI tried reading college physics about degenerate gas!

Kept stepped in: *'Nidhi is in 3rd grade.'* It gave me a fun balloon analogy, and suggested a space book not already on my shelf."

---

### Part 4 • Scenario 3: Saving Money & Hardware
**DAD:**  
"And when I went to buy an HDMI switch, Kept paused checkout: *'Your TV already has open ports, and you have an unopened 4K cable in your drawer.'*

So I didn’t waste $35 on something we already had."

---

### Part 5 • The Summary & Close
**DAD:**  
"AI has all the world's knowledge, but zero home context. Kept is the personal layer that connects you and your family to Alexa."

**NIDHI:**  
"Kept trains the AI's brain on our family's safety, learning, and real needs.  
**AI finds it. Kept personalizes it.**"

**NIDHI & DAD (together):**  
"Kept turns a generic assistant into a household guardian. Thank you, Amazon!"
